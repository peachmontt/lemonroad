Lemon Road Reward Rules

These rules govern daily free gameplay rewards in Lemon Road. Please read them carefully before participating. Rules may change at any time.

Basic Rules

  • Lemon Road is free to play. No purchase or deposit is required to play.
  • No purchase or deposit is required to be eligible for free player rewards.
  • Daily rewards may be distributed based on leaderboard results, random selection, special events, or published reward rules.
  • Rewards are not guaranteed.
  • Reward rules may change at any time without prior notice.
  • The reward pool size may vary or may be zero on some days.

Eligibility

To be eligible for daily rewards, players must:

  • Complete at least one valid gameplay run during the reward period
  • Have a registered score on the daily leaderboard (if leaderboard rewards are active)
  • Not be disqualified for any reason listed under the anti-cheat section below
  • Meet any additional eligibility criteria specified in the current daily rules

Reward Distribution

Daily rewards may be distributed in one or more of the following ways:

  • Leaderboard rewards: Players ranked in the top positions on the daily leaderboard may receive a share of the reward pool
  • Random selection: Free players may be randomly selected from eligible participants regardless of their score
  • Challenge rewards: Players who complete specific daily challenges may be eligible
  • Event rewards: Special game events may have their own reward rules published separately

The specific distribution method for each day will be announced in the game or on official channels where applicable.

Anti-Cheat and Disqualification

Players may be disqualified from reward eligibility for:

  • Using bots, automation scripts, or any non-human input
  • Exploiting game bugs or glitches to achieve an artificially high score
  • Using duplicate accounts or farming rewards across multiple accounts
  • Score manipulation by any technical means
  • Suspicious activity patterns that indicate automated or fraudulent gameplay
  • Any behavior determined by the Lemon Road team to violate fair play

Disqualification is at the sole discretion of the Lemon Road team. Disqualified scores will be removed from the leaderboard.

Final Determination

Final reward eligibility is determined by the Lemon Road system and team. All decisions are final. Lemon Road reserves the right to modify, pause, or cancel daily rewards at any time.

Important Disclaimer

Lemon Road is a free game with daily reward events. It is not an investment product, financial service, or guaranteed income source. Rewards are part of the game experience. Playing Lemon Road should not be treated as a substitute for financial planning.

Daily rewards are not guaranteed. Players should not make financial decisions based on expected rewards from Lemon Road gameplay.
