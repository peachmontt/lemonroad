Install Lemon Road on Android

Android Chrome supports one-tap PWA install. Add Lemon Road to your home screen for full-screen gameplay and daily prize reminders.

Chrome detected your install intent. You can install Lemon Road right now:

  1. Step 1 — Open in Chrome

    Open https://www.lemonroad.xyz in the Chrome app on your Android device.

  2. Step 2 — Tap the three-dot menu

    Tap the ⋮ three-dot menu in the top-right corner of Chrome.

  3. Step 3 — Tap "Install app"

    Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen" from the menu. Chrome may also show a small install banner at the bottom of the screen.

  4. Step 4 — Confirm

    Tap "Install" or "Add" in the prompt. Lemon Road will be added to your home screen and app drawer.

  5. Step 5 — Open from your home screen

    Tap the Lemon Road icon on your home screen or in your app drawer. It opens full-screen with no browser bar.

Enable daily prize reminders

After installing, Lemon Road can send you a reminder when the daily prize draw is live. You'll be asked to enable notifications when you open the app from your home screen.
