Install Lemon Road on iPhone

iPhone requires adding web apps manually from Safari. It only takes 4 taps — and once it's on your home screen, you get full-screen gameplay and can enable daily prize reminders.

You're not using Safari. iPhone players must use Safari to add Lemon Road to the home screen.

Copy the link below and paste it into Safari:

https://www.lemonroad.xyz

  1. Step 1 — Open in Safari

    Open https://www.lemonroad.xyz in the Safari app on your iPhone. The steps below will not work in Chrome, Instagram, or other in-app browsers.

  2. Step 2 — Tap the Share button

    Tap the Share button at the bottom centre of Safari — it looks like a box with an arrow pointing up.

  3. Step 3 — Tap "Add to Home Screen"

    Scroll down in the share sheet and tap "Add to Home Screen". It shows a small plus icon next to a square.

  4. Step 4 — Tap "Add"

    Tap "Add" in the top-right corner to confirm. The Lemon Road icon will appear on your home screen.

  5. Step 5 — Open from your home screen

    Tap the Lemon Road icon on your home screen. It opens full-screen — no browser bar — and you can then enable daily prize reminders.

Enable daily prize reminders

After installing, open Lemon Road from your home screen and you'll be able to enable notifications so we can remind you when the daily prize draw is live.
