Lemon Road Leaderboard

The Lemon Road leaderboard tracks top daily survival scores from free gameplay. Players compete by guiding the lemon down the road, surviving obstacles, and reaching the highest score before the daily reset.

Play Now to Climb the Leaderboard

Today's Top Scores

Live leaderboard scores are displayed inside the game. Open Lemon Road and check the leaderboard panel on the start screen to see today's top players and their survival distances.

Scores are updated in real time as players complete runs. The leaderboard shows player names (or anonymous IDs) and their best survival score for the current day.

How Rankings Work

Players are ranked by their highest single-run survival score in the current day. Your best run counts — not an average of all runs. If you play multiple times in a day, only your highest score appears on the leaderboard.

The leaderboard is sorted from highest score to lowest. Ties are broken by the time of the submission — the earlier score ranks higher.

Daily Reset Time

The leaderboard resets every 24 hours at midnight UTC. When the day resets, all scores are cleared and a new daily competition begins. Players start fresh each day, which means anyone can climb to the top with a great run.

Daily Reward Eligibility

Players at the top of the leaderboard may be eligible for daily rewards, depending on current reward rules. Leaderboard placement does not automatically guarantee a reward. Final reward eligibility is subject to the published reward rules, reward pool availability, and anti-cheat review.

Anti-Cheat Review

All leaderboard submissions are subject to anti-cheat review. Scores from bots, automation, score manipulation, duplicate accounts, or exploit behavior will be removed and the player may be disqualified from reward eligibility.
