What Kind of Game Is Lemon Road?

Lemon Road is a casual survival game with a meme twist. You control a lemon moving down a chaotic road. Your goal is to stay on the road, avoid obstacles, and survive as long as possible. The longer you survive, the higher your score.

It's fast, funny, and easy to share. Most runs last under a minute — which makes it perfect for quick play sessions at any time of day.
