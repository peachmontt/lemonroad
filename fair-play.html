Fair Play and Anti-Cheat

Lemon Road (lemonroad) uses fair play rules to protect the daily leaderboard and reward system in the lemonroad game. Players may be removed from leaderboard or reward eligibility for bots, automation, duplicate abuse, exploits, suspicious activity, or attempts to manipulate scores.

Fair Gameplay

Lemon Road is a skill-based survival game. Fair play means every player competes on equal terms — using only their own hands (or tilt) to control the lemon. The leaderboard reflects genuine human skill and survival ability.

Lemon Road is committed to keeping the competition fair for all players, especially when daily rewards are involved.

Leaderboard Integrity

The daily leaderboard is the foundation of the reward system. To protect its integrity, Lemon Road monitors all score submissions for suspicious patterns. Scores that are statistically impossible, inconsistent with normal gameplay, or submitted by automated tools are removed.

The leaderboard is reviewed before daily rewards are distributed. Players on the leaderboard at the time of reward distribution may still be disqualified if their scores are flagged during review.

Daily Reward Eligibility

Players must meet fair play requirements to be eligible for daily rewards. Meeting a leaderboard position is necessary but not sufficient for reward eligibility. Scores must also pass anti-cheat review and meet any additional criteria specified in the current reward rules.

Bot and Exploit Policy

The following are explicitly prohibited and will result in disqualification:

  • Using bots, scripts, or automated tools to play the game
  • Modifying the game client, injecting code, or altering game state
  • Using browser extensions that interfere with game input or scoring
  • Exploiting known or unknown game bugs to achieve abnormal scores
  • Automating device tilt or touch input to simulate gameplay
  • Using multiple devices simultaneously to inflate gameplay metrics

Score Review

Lemon Road reserves the right to review any score at any time. Scores may be reviewed based on automatic detection, manual report, or routine audit. Players flagged for review may have their scores temporarily withheld from reward eligibility pending outcome.

Disqualification Rules

Players may be disqualified from the leaderboard and/or reward eligibility for:

  • Any prohibited behavior listed in the Bot and Exploit Policy
  • Operating duplicate or shared accounts
  • Attempting to manipulate the reward pool or reward eligibility system
  • Abusive behavior toward other players or the platform
  • Any behavior deemed to violate fair play by the Lemon Road team

Disqualification decisions are final. Disqualified scores are removed from the leaderboard. Rewards already distributed will not be reversed for gameplay issues discovered after distribution.
