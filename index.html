Lemon Road

Free Meme Survival Game with Daily Rewards

Lemon Road (also known as lemonroad) is a free browser-based survival game where you guide a lemon down a chaotic road, avoid obstacles, survive as long as possible, and climb the daily leaderboard. Some call it the road to a million — your million dollar road starts here. Play on desktop with your mouse or on mobile with touch and motion controls. Top players and lucky free players can compete for daily rewards.

Play Free

Free to play. No deposit required. Daily rewards are based on gameplay rules and leaderboard results.

How Lemon Road Works

Start the game, control the lemon, stay on the road, collect points, avoid chaos, and try to survive longer than other players. Your score can place you on the daily leaderboard.

Daily Rewards

Lemon Road includes daily rewards for free gameplay. Players can compete through skill, survival time, leaderboard ranking, and daily game rules. Rewards are not guaranteed and may change based on the current reward pool.
